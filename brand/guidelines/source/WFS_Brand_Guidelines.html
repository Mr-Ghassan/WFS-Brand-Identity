Logo concept · v2

Five fields,
one aperture

The WFS icon is an original, parametric aperture. It keeps the approved idea — separate financial fields converging into one controlled view — and rebuilds it with perfect geometry: exact five-fold symmetry and nothing but circular arcs.

  • Precision — every curve is an arc of one of seven circles; every tip is a true tangent.
  • Balance — rotational symmetry means the mark is equally weighted at every size.
  • Depth — the premium version adds light and soft contact shadows, built from gradients only.
Concept study that defined the idea (retired artwork and colours).

The icon

Five fields.
One aperture.

The icon is an aperture of five interlocking blades. Each blade is a curvilinear surface running from the outer field into the central opening — separate operations resolving into one controlled view.

  • Tonal ramp clockwise: B1 Mint · B2 Teal · B3 Teal 700 · B4 Deep Teal · B5 WFS Dark.
  • Exact 72° rotational symmetry; outer circle and aperture share one centre.
  • Aperture Ø = 0.38 × icon Ø — it is also the clear-space unit.
  • Blade tips meet the aperture tangentially; never round or cut them.
  • On dark grounds use the Inverse ramp (Mint 100 Teal 800).

Clear space

Measured by
the aperture

The clear-space unit A is the width of the icon's central aperture (0.380 × icon diameter). Keep at least 1 A free of text, edges and other graphics on every side of any lockup or of the icon.

  • Minimum: 1 A on all sides.
  • Preferred (brand-led layouts): 2 A.
  • UI chrome (headers, nav bars): 0.5 A is permitted when the logo is vertically centred in a bar of 2.2 × icon height.
  • Text: body text or headings never enter the clear-space zone; align text to the zone edge, not the artwork.

Because A is part of the icon, clear space scales automatically with the logo — no pixel values needed.

Premium display

Depth without decoration

The premium version uses the same master geometry. Each blade carries a gradient from a shaded tip to a lit rim; every overlap casts a soft contact shadow that follows the edge circle exactly; the upper blade catches a fine lit edge; a key light falls from the top-left. All of it is built with gradients — no raster effects.

  • Use for: launch visuals, keynote title slides, event screens, store artwork, hero sections.
  • Never use for: UI chrome, favicons, documents, small sizes (< 64 px / 20 mm), one-colour print.
  • No chrome, no glare, no bevel, no drop shadow. No gold or blue.

Typeface · Arabic

Myriad Arabic

مايرى عربي

The approved Arabic typeface is Myriad Arabic (Adobe; PostScript family MyriadArabic-*). Its humanist sans construction pairs with Instrument Sans in stroke contrast, openness and colour on the page.

  • Arabic runs right-to-left; layouts mirror, numerals and brand names stay LTR.
  • Set Arabic ~ 1.15 × the Latin size for equal optical weight; line height 1.6.
  • Never letter-space Arabic; never apply faux bold or italic.
  • Use Western digits (0–9) in financial UI for global consistency; Eastern Arabic digits only on user locale request.
Arabic specimens in this edition are rendered with a labelled proxy (Noto Sans Arabic) because the licensed Myriad Arabic font file was not available in the build environment. Place the Myriad Arabic files in the font directory and rebuild — the layout switches automatically. The approved Arabic typeface remains Myriad Arabic.
