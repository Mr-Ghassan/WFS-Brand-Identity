A global financial operating system that gives modern organisations a unified view and controlled execution of their financial operations.

These guidelines define version 1.0 of the WFS identity: the approved icon and wordmark rebuilt as precise vector masters, the Concept A colour family, Instrument Sans and Myriad Arabic typography, and a graphic language derived entirely from the icon. They are written for product, marketing and partner teams — and for anyone building with WFS.

Logo concept

Approved form,
rebuilt with precision

The approved icon and wordmark define the geometry and character of WFS. The metallic gold/blue rendering was a presentation effect, not the identity. For version 1.0 both marks were reconstructed as clean vectors and translated into the WFS teal palette.

  • Icon — five-blade aperture rebuilt from circles only: 10 boundary arcs + 5 biarc edges (G1). Silhouette IoU vs approved art 0.992.
  • Wordmark — traced at 3× super-sampling, straight strokes snapped to true horizontals/verticals, curves optimised. IoU 0.997.
  • Colour — gold/silver/graphite materials mapped to Mint, Teal and Deep tones. Old Concept C colours are retired.

The icon

Five fields.
One aperture.

The icon is an aperture of five interlocking blades. Each blade is a curvilinear surface running from the outer field into the central opening — separate operations resolving into one controlled view.

  • B1 light blade · Mint   B2, B4 principal blades · Teal
  • B3 shadow blade · Deep Teal   B5 base blade · WFS Dark
  • Outer silhouette: five arcs (layered, not a perfect circle) — keep it.
  • The aperture is off-centre by design (+2.1 %, −2.4 % of D).
  • Blade tips are sharp; never round them.

The wordmark

Custom letterforms — never typeset

Ribbon construction

W, F and S are drawn as continuous folded ribbons: sheared diagonals, a sweeping F bar and an S built from two horizontal bands.

Geometry

Stroke angles 60° / 63.5° / 120° / 123° / 125°; true horizontals and verticals; cap height = 1000 master units; width 4 680.

Relationship to type

Instrument Sans supports the wordmark; it never replaces it. Do not recreate "WFS" in any font.

Colour

WFS Dark (#022329) on light, Off-White (#F3F5F2) on dark. One colour only — the premium version is for display use.

Clear space

Measured by
the aperture

The clear-space unit A is the width of the icon's central aperture (0.406 × icon diameter). Keep at least 1 A free of text, edges and other graphics on every side of any lockup or of the icon.

  • Minimum: 1 A on all sides.
  • Preferred (brand-led layouts): 2 A.
  • UI chrome (headers, nav bars): 0.5 A is permitted when the logo is vertically centred in a bar of 2.2 × icon height.
  • Text: body text or headings never enter the clear-space zone; align text to the zone edge, not the artwork.

Because A is part of the icon, clear space scales automatically with the logo — no pixel values needed.

Depth without decoration

The premium version uses the same master geometry with controlled teal gradients, a key light from the top-right, fine lit edges on the upper blade and in-hue shade on the lower blade at each overlap.

Typeface · Arabic

Myriad Arabic

مايرى عربي

The approved Arabic typeface is Myriad Arabic (Adobe; PostScript family MyriadArabic-*). Its humanist sans construction pairs with Instrument Sans in stroke contrast, openness and colour on the page.

  • Arabic runs right-to-left; layouts mirror, numerals and brand names stay LTR.
  • Set Arabic ~ 1.15 × the Latin size for equal optical weight; line height 1.6.
  • Never letter-space Arabic; never apply faux bold or italic.
  • Use Western digits (0–9) in financial UI for global consistency; Eastern Arabic digits only on user locale request.
Arabic specimens in this edition are rendered with a labelled proxy (Noto Sans Arabic) because the licensed Myriad Arabic font file was not available in the build environment. Place the Myriad Arabic files in the font directory and rebuild — the layout switches automatically. The approved Arabic typeface remains Myriad Arabic.

Numbers are the brand

Financial typography

  • Tabular lining figures (font-feature-settings: "tnum", "lnum") in every table, KPI, statement and chart axis.
  • Right-align numeric columns on the decimal; fixed decimals per currency (USD 2 · OMR 3 · JPY 0).
  • Negatives in parentheses in statements; a true minus (−, U+2212) in UI deltas. Colour is never the only signal.
  • Currency as ISO code after the amount (48,612,480.25 USD); symbols only in local consumer contexts.
  • Percentages with one decimal and a thin space before % in print; sign always shown for change (+4.9 %).
  • Large values abbreviate only in charts and tiles (48.6M), never in ledgers.
  • Hierarchy: KPI values SemiBold, labels Regular in Slate 600, units in Slate 400.
